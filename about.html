About Me

Mechanical Engineering Student

I design and build mechanical systems with precision and purpose. As a Mechanical Engineering student at UC Riverside, I transform concepts into tangible solutions -- from aerodynamic front wings tested in CFD to HiFi speakers that deliver pristine audio through custom 3D-printed enclosures.

My approach blends computational tools (ANSYS, SolidWorks) with hands-on prototyping (3D printing, CNC machining) to create systems that perform. I believe great engineering bridges the gap between digital design and physical reality.

Focus Areas: Aerodynamic design, CAD/CAE simulation, rapid prototyping, and experimental testing. I work at the intersection of theory and practice, using computational fluid dynamics to validate physical designs before they ever touch a manufacturing tool.

Design Philosophy: Every CAD model is validated. From GD&T tolerances to CFD convergence criteria -- details define performance. Precision, innovation, and performance drive every project I undertake.
